HOW THE PROJECT COMMUNICATES
  The policy talks to Gazebo directly over gz-transport (Gazebo's own publish/subscribe middleware, Python bindings
  in tools/gzsim.py), not through ROS 2 topics. No ros_gz bridge is started, so the ROS 2 graph (last section) only
  holds ROS's defaults. Per robot rN (N = 1..4): lidar + odometry in, drive command out; one overhead camera shared.
  Control loop: every 0.1 s of sim time (20 physics steps of 5 ms) read sensors -> policy -> safety layer -> cmd_vel.
